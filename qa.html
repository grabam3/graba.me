Where creative software breaks

Generic QA and AI-written tests rarely check these. Your users find them on stage, mid-session or on release day.

MIDI

Devices plugged in or pulled out mid-session, clock sync, channel and note edge cases.

Browser audio

Autoplay rules, suspended audio contexts, Safari quirks, background tabs.

Mobile interruptions

Phone calls, Bluetooth headphones, switching output devices, the app going to the background.

Offline and flaky networks

Shows, studios and basements with bad signal. What still works, and what fails quietly.

Plus the usual: sign-ups, payments, forms, accessibility, across browsers and devices.
Audio timing, sample rates, plugin hosts and file handling: on request.

Bug report

Example
Title
Playback stops when switching to Bluetooth headphones mid-song
Severity
High blocks playback, no error shown
Steps
1. Start any track on speakers · 2. Connect Bluetooth headphones · 3. Press play again
Expected
Playback continues on the headphones
Actual
Silent; play button shows "playing"; reload needed
Environment
Chrome on Android · web build from your staging link
Evidence
Screen recording, console log
