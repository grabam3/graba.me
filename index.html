How it works

You leave with the files and the notes to do it again without me.

  1. Free intro call

    20 minutes to hear what you want at the end and check your gear.

  2. Price in writing

    Small jobs get booked at the listed rate. Bigger ones get a written scope and quote first.

  3. Session

    We work side by side on your gear or mine, in person or over video.

  4. Log and handover

    What changed, how it was tested and how to undo it, written down and handed to you with the files.
